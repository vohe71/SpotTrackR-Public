2. General information on data processing

Personal data is processed only to the extent necessary to provide a functioning app together with its content and services. Processing takes place exclusively within the framework of the applicable legal provisions (GDPR, BDSG, TDDDG).

Almost all of the processing described in this policy takes place exclusively locally on your device. Data that does not leave your device is neither collected nor stored nor evaluated by the Provider.

3. No registration, no user account

The App requires no registration and no login. No names, e-mail addresses, telephone numbers or other account data are collected or stored by the controller. No profiling and no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

4. Data created in the app

4.1 Saved spots, waypoints, routes and settings

When you occupy a slot or set a waypoint, the App stores the position determined at that moment — latitude and longitude, altitude where available, the location accuracy reported by the operating system, and the time of the measurement. In addition, it stores the names, symbols and colours you choose for the slots, any named saved routes, your settings (units, north reference, presentation, haptics, screen behaviour) and a note as to whether the introduction has already been shown.

This data is held in the App's local store (the App's system preferences store) and in a storage area shared by the App and its share extension (app group). Both areas reside exclusively on your device. The shared area is required so that a spot accepted via the share function arrives in your grid.

Saved positions are location data and may therefore constitute personal data. This data is not transmitted to the Provider. The Provider has no access to this content.

Legal basis: Insofar as this involves any processing by the controller at all, it takes place in order to perform the usage contract pursuant to Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

4.2 No synchronisation; inclusion in device backups

The App synchronises nothing. There is no iCloud synchronisation, no transfer between multiple devices, and no storage in a cloud database of the Provider or of a third party.

If you have set up a backup on your device (iCloud backup or a backup on your computer), the App's locally stored data may — like the data of other apps — become part of that backup. The backup is a function of your device; Apple and you yourself are responsible for it. The Provider has no access to backups. You control this in the system settings (Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → iCloud Backup).

4.3 Sharing and receiving spots

Using your device's share function, you can pass on a saved spot. In doing so, the coordinates, and where available the altitude, accuracy and time of measurement, as well as the name you gave the slot, leave your device — depending on the channel chosen, as one of the App's own files, as text containing the coordinates, or as a link to Apple Maps. Where this information ends up is determined solely by you through your choice of recipient, target app or target service; the respective provider is responsible for processing it there. Nothing is transmitted to the Provider of the App when you share.

Conversely, you can accept spots that reach you — as one of the App's files, via the share extension (e.g. from Apple Maps), via a map link, or via coordinates in a message. The processing takes place on your device; an accepted spot is treated like a spot you saved yourself (section 4.1).

Please bear in mind: a shared spot discloses a position. Only pass spots on to recipients who are meant to have that information.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (provision of the App function you invoked).

5. Device functions and system permissions

5.1 Location

The App asks for permission to access your device's location while it is in use ("While Using the App"). The location is used to save a spot or waypoint, to calculate the direction and distance to a saved spot, to show your position and heading on the map, and to detect arrival at a spot.

The following applies:

  • Location determination runs only while the App is open in the foreground. There is no background location tracking, no continuous recording of routes and no creation of movement profiles. The only things recorded are what you yourself save as a spot or waypoint.
  • The location data is not transmitted to the Provider or to any third party.
  • For usable guidance, the App needs your precise location. If you have granted only the approximate location, the App asks — via the system dialogue — for temporary permission to use the precise location. The operating system grants this permission for a limited time; the App cannot extend it.
  • After saving a spot, the App continues measuring for a few seconds and replaces the stored value if a more accurate measurement of the same place becomes available. The measurements discarded in the process are not stored permanently.

Granting the permission is voluntary. Without location permission, the App cannot perform its core function — saving a spot and finding your way back.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent, given via the operating system's permission dialogue).

Withdrawal: At any time via the system settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → SpotTrackR). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out up to that point.

5.2 Camera

On devices with a camera, the App can overlay the direction to your target on the live camera image. For this purpose, the App requests the system permission for the camera the first time you open this view.

The following applies:

  • The camera image is shown live on the screen only. It is not recorded, not stored, not analysed and not transmitted — neither to the Provider nor to any third party.
  • No image recognition, face recognition or object recognition takes place. The overlay is calculated solely from location and attitude data.
  • When you leave the view, the camera is shut down immediately.

Granting the permission is voluntary. Without camera permission, the App remains fully usable; only the camera overlay is unavailable.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent, given via the operating system's permission dialogue).

Withdrawal: At any time via the system settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → SpotTrackR).

5.3 Compass and motion sensors

For the direction indicator, the App uses the device's magnetic compass as well as its attitude and motion sensors. These values are processed only transiently in memory, in order to orient the arrow, the compass, the map and the camera overlay. They are not stored and not transmitted. No separate system permission is required for this.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

5.4 No access to photos, contacts, calendar, microphone, health data or notifications

The App requests no access to your photo library, your contacts, your calendar, your reminders, your microphone, your address book or health data. The App sends no notifications and does not request the notification permission. No audio or video recordings are made.
